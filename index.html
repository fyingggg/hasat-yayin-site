Hasat Yayin

Hasat Yayin is a small internal tool made by Kafei Stüdyo (Kafei Studio), an independent game studio. It publishes our own marketing videos for the game Harvest Atlas (Hasat Atlası) to our own social media accounts, including our own YouTube channel.

What it does

  • Uploads videos that we created, with our own titles and descriptions, to our own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API.
  • Reads basic information about that same channel (its name and its recent uploads) to confirm that an upload succeeded.

The tool is not a public service. It has a single user, the studio owner, and no accounts, sign-up, or advertising. It does not collect data about visitors or about other YouTube users.

This tool uses YouTube API Services. See our Privacy Policy.
