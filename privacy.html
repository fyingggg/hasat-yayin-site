Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026 · Terms of Service

Hasat Yayin ("the tool") is an internal tool operated by Kafei Stüdyo ("we"). It is used only by the studio owner to publish our own videos to our own YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

YouTube API Services

The tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it, the studio owner is bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What we access

With the permission of the Google account that owns the channel, the tool requests two scopes: youtube.upload (to upload our videos to our channel) and youtube.readonly (to read our own channel's name, ID, and recent uploads to confirm that an upload succeeded and to avoid duplicates). We do not access data about other YouTube users, their videos, comments, or subscribers.

What we store

  • An OAuth refresh token, in a local file on the studio's computer that only the owner can read. It is never sent to any server other than Google's.
  • For each uploaded video, its ID and URL in a local job record.

We do not copy YouTube data to any database or website, and we do not use it for advertising, profiling, or analytics.

Sharing

We do not sell, share, or transfer YouTube API data to third parties.

Revoking access and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. When access is revoked, or when the tool is retired, we delete the stored refresh token and any stored YouTube API data (the video IDs and URLs in the local job record) within 30 days. You can also ask us to delete any data we hold by writing to the address below; we will do so within 30 days. Revoking access also stops Google from issuing further tokens to the tool. Google's own handling of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Visitors to this website

This website is static. It uses no cookies, scripts, or analytics, and we do not collect data about visitors. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do. Questions: kafei.studyo@gmail.com.
